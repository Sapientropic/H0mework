# Theory Becomes an Occurrence

> **Editorial draft - three-page second-round flagship.** The Σ (theory-adoption) face turns
> performativity into a responsibility rule without treating every unpublished idea as a world event.

## Page 1 of 3 - An engine cannot remain outside its own world

A theory may begin as presentation. Once it is adopted by a model, policy, market, classifier,
institution, or technical infrastructure and changes what people can do, it becomes an occurrence.

```text
theory released
→ adopted by an institutional carrier
→ categories, incentives, prices, or routes change
→ beneficiaries and hidden bearers are generated
→ residuals and maintenance debt appear
→ the theory enters the same ledger as its effects
```

The Σ rule is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{world-changing theory}
\Longrightarrow
\text{source-backed accountable occurrence}.
}
\]

This does not prohibit abstraction. It prohibits a theory from acting as an engine while claiming the
immunity of a camera. Predictive models, economic theories, audit metrics, eligibility rules, and
formal proofs remain free as presentations until an adoption event gives them institutional effect.

The adoption event - not the abstract proposition alone - generates responsibility.

## Page 2 of 3 - The performativity ledger

An adopted theory must expose:

- the source and publication or deployment event;
- the organization that installed it;
- the classifications, routes, prices, or decisions it changed;
- the lineages that gained new standing or access;
- the bearers pushed into its observer kernel;
- model, data, and institutional maintenance debt;
- challenge, exit, and revision routes;
- downstream consumer and generated next.

```text
adoption
├─ visible success readout
├─ changed incentives and behaviour
├─ new data generated by the theory's own use
├─ hidden residual bearers
└─ revised environment for the next evaluation
```

The observer therefore becomes reflexive. A model evaluated only on the data regime created by its
own adoption cannot treat that regime as independent evidence. The resulting feedback must remain in
the occurrence lineage.

The same rule applies to this framework's completed root grammar, `M∞`. If it is adopted in policy,
software, or organizational practice, that adoption becomes a Σ occurrence. The framework must
account for its own beneficiaries, classification effects, exclusions, maintenance debt, and
possible failure.

This is already machine-instantiated, not a future schema. M13 installs a source-backed causal
theory adoption on the existing renewal/runtime splice: exact source seed, effect, whole-ledger row,
independent consumer, and generated next share one occurrence. M∞ stores that M13 certificate, and
canonical M14 stores the M∞ foundation.

The installed machine chain is:

- `M13WholeProgramRegression.m13WholeProgramConsumer` - the whole sink adds observer

What remains external is a particular field deployment with its empirical adoption events and data,
not the generic Σ carrier, consumer, feedback runtime, or revision chain.

The decisive question is whether this carrier can distinguish harmless description, causal
intervention, self-fulfilling adoption, counter-performative failure, and strategic gaming without
letting an observer decide its own authority.

> A theory may describe the world. Once its adoption changes the world, it must become answerable for
> the transition it helped generate.
