> **Editorial draft - four-page flagship.** UBI is one infrastructure for the constitutional right

## Page 1 of 4 - The ability to say no

Universal basic income is commonly described as a recurring cash transfer. That description sees
the payment but not the future it is meant to keep open.

The process-generated object is:

\[
\text{standing lineage}
+\text{periodic settlement}
\longrightarrow
\text{purchasing-power standing}
\longrightarrow
\text{role-independent actionable continuation}.
\]

An exit is actionable when the bearer can leave the relation under examination and still inhabit at
least one non-idle continuation. The alternative must not be supplied by the employer, household,
landlord, platform, creditor, or administrative classification being refused.

The sixth constitutional prohibition therefore precedes UBI:

> No standing without a real capacity to say no.

UBI is a flagship implementation because periodic, universal settlement can renew that capacity
without requiring the bearer to accept another role first.

## Page 3 of 4 - UBI changes power geometry

The primary political effect is not a larger consumption aggregate. It is a change in the outside
option of employment, household, housing, debt, platform, and bureaucratic relations.

“Unconditional” means that renewal is not conditioned on employment, a poverty classification, or a
moral-performance score. It does not mean source-free. Payment, acceptance, bearer, consumer,
residual, and next must still occur.

UBI, universal basic services, and a job guarantee remain typed-distinct:

| Institution | What it generates | What it cannot impersonate |
|---|---|---|
| UBI | purchasing-power standing outside a role | housing, health, transport, or care capacity |
| universal basic services | material service slots and accessible routes | role-independent income standing |
| job guarantee | standing inside a new role | an outside option after refusing that role |

All three may coexist. A new role cannot be used as proof that the bearer was free to refuse roles
altogether. A payment cannot be used as proof that housing, health, or transport capacity exists.

The formal result establishes this encoded structural contract. Inflation paths, labour-supply
responses, fiscal regimes, political coalitions, and empirical adequacy remain actual occurrences to
be measured and accounted for.
