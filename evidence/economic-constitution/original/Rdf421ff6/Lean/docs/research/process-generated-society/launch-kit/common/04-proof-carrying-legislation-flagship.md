## Page 1 of 4 - Put the burden on power

Modern legislation normally presents a text, a jurisdiction, and a procedure of enactment. Its
maintenance burden, hidden residuals, exit conditions, failed classifications, claim migration, and
downstream consumer are often discovered only after it changes the world.

Proof-Carrying Legislation reverses the burden:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{ProofCarryingPower}
\quad\text{not}\quad
\operatorname{ProofCarryingPersonhood}.
}
\]

worthy enough to possess standing. A coercive classification, tax, entitlement, property claim,
platform gate, automated decision, or compulsory settlement must expose the provenance and
consequences of its own authority.

applicant and not promises supplied by the authority being audited.
